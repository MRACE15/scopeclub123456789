Who We Are

Inside SCOPE

SCOPE is a student-run technical club dedicated to bridging the gap between classroom learning and industry-ready skills.

Our Mission

Cultivate passionate developers, problem-solvers, and creators who push the boundaries of technology.

Our Community

A diverse group of students from all years, united by curiosity and a love for building things that matter.

Our Vision

Be the platform where students transform ideas into real-world projects and launch their tech careers.

Our Activities

What We Do

From hackathons to workshops, SCOPE keeps you in the loop on everything tech.

01

Hackathons

24–48 hour build sessions. Ideate, design, and ship real solutions. Prizes, mentors, and pure energy.

  • Team-based problem solving
  • Industry mentors on hand
  • Cash prizes & recognition
02

Coding Contests

Regular competitive programming challenges on LeetCode and Codeforces to sharpen your thinking.

  • Weekly coding challenges
  • Leaderboards & rankings
  • Interview prep focus
03

Projects

Collaborative long-term projects across web, AI/ML, mobile, and cloud. Build a portfolio that stands out.

  • Mentored project tracks
  • GitHub-based collaboration
  • Showcase at semester end
04

Workshops

Hands-on learning sessions covering AWS, React, Python, ML, and more. Expert-led. Free resources.

  • Expert-led sessions
  • Live coding demos
  • Free learning resources
